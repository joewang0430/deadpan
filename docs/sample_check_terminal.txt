## How do I boil an egg?

### base #1

Boiling an egg is simple and can be done in a few easy steps. Here's how to do it perfectly:

### Method: Boiling a Hard-Boiled Egg (Classic)

**Ingredients & Tools:**
- 1 egg (fresh is best)
- A saucepan
- Water
- A spoon or fork
- A timer (optional but helpful)

**Steps:**

1. **Place the egg in a saucepan**  
   Add the egg to a medium-sized saucepan.

2. **Add cold water**  
   Fill the saucepan with enough cold water to cover the egg by about 1 inch (2.5 cm).  
   *Tip: Starting with cold water helps prevent cracking and ensures even cooking.*

3. **Bring to a boil**  
   Place the saucepan on the stove and turn the heat to high.  
   Let the water come to a rolling boil.

4. **Reduce heat and cover**  
   Once boiling, reduce the heat to low or medium-low (to maintain a gentle simmer).  
   Cover the saucepan with a lid.

5. **Cook for the desired time**  
   - **Soft-boiled (yolk still runny):** 4–5 minutes  
   - **Medium-boiled (yolk slightly set):** 6–7 minutes  
   - **Hard-boiled (fully set yolk):** 9–12 minutes  

   *Note: The exact time may vary slightly depending on your stove and egg size.*

6. **Cool the egg**  
   After cooking, remove the pan from the heat and immediately transfer the eggs to a bowl of ice water.  
   Let it sit for 5–10 minutes. This stops the cooking process and makes the egg easier to peel.

7. **Peel and serve**  
   Gently tap the egg on a hard surface to crack the shell, then peel under running water if needed.
